TEAM CALCULATIONS

For each team maintain:

remainingPoints
usedPoints
playersBought

Logic:

usedPoints = sum of player bids
remainingPoints = pointsPerTeam - usedPoints

playersRemainingSlots = playersPerTeam - playersBought

MAX BID CALCULATION

Max allowed bid must be calculated as:

maxBid = remainingPoints - ((remainingSlots - 1) * minimumBid)

This ensures a team can still buy minimum players for remaining slots.

Example:

remainingPoints = 1000
remainingSlots = 5
minimumBid = 50

maxBid = 1000 - (4 × 50) = 800

Display this in the Overview table.
